CHAIPOINT×MAGGI

Decomplex the brief.

Chtrsocial — Social Media Manager 09/2024 — 09/2025 Team of 5
The Brief

Chaipoint × Maggi.
A new menu collaboration.

Chaipoint was adding a new menu item to its lineup — a collaboration with Maggi — and needed content that made the launch feel less like an advertisement and more like something people already knew and loved.

The Problem

How do you make a product collaboration feel culturally relevant — rather than like an advertisement?

The Thought

DECOMPLEX
THE BRIEF.

The Human Truth
Chai + Maggi = comfort.
Chai + Maggi = nostalgia.
Chai + Maggi = best friends.
The Creative Direction
RELATABILITY>COMPLEXITY

The content leaned all the way into that truth — chai and Maggi, cast as best friends, in the kind of everyday moment everyone already recognises.

About

A LITTLE ABOUT
THE PERSON BEHIND
THE CAMPAIGNS.

I'm happiest at the intersection of people, culture, brands and business — the point where a consumer insight turns into a piece of content, and a piece of content turns into a number someone in a boardroom actually cares about.

Seven years in, I've moved from making the post to owning the strategy, the team and the budget behind it — across chai stalls, resort chains, skincare shelves and celebrity shoots. Different categories keep the thinking sharp; the throughline is always the same obsession — finding the idea that makes people feel something before they realise it's marketing.

I started out making content for a living, and somewhere along the way ended up running the whole system behind it — brand, budget, performance and the people who make it happen.

Most of my work sits in categories you can eat, drink, or stay in — F&B, hospitality and lifestyle brands where the experience is the product, and marketing has to do more than just show up.

I like ideas that are easy to say and hard to copy. And I still can't walk past a badly designed menu without wanting to fix it.
